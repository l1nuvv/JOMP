# Just One More Peel store copy

- Start from the [existing itch.io draft](../../../docs/ITCH_PAGE_DRAFT.md), [release status](../../../docs/ROADMAP.md) and evidence for the target build. When editing a specific passage, preserve the surrounding copy and the author's tone.
- Open with the player's repeatable actions and the reward visible in the target build. Tie tool upgrades to what they let the player do. Use concrete language from the game and protect story details the player should uncover while playing.
- Keep RU and EN equivalent in meaning. Use each language's actual menu labels and control bindings. Check the executable name and prerequisites against the verified release artifact when editing download instructions.
- Ground claims about play time, system requirements, content and features in the project's [playtest](../../../docs/PLAYTEST.md), [performance](../../../docs/PERFORMANCE.md) and release evidence. Flag unsupported details in review notes and keep them out of public copy.
