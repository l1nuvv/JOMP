## Visual interpretation of owner screenshots — 2026-10-28
The supplied gameplay frames show stylized 3D, with visibly low-poly forms in some objects and smoother rounded forms in others. Low polygon count is not itself the visual target. The owner wants this overall appealing cartoon character for our own courtyard, not a copy of either game.

- **Forms:** recognisable, substantial silhouettes, simplified shapes and selected rounded edges. Close-up tools need coherent constructed details and convincing contact; do not present crude boxes or primitive piles as finished art.
- **Materials and palette:** broad, clear colour areas and simplified surface detail; distinguish paper, plaster and tools without relying on photorealistic wear. Use a coherent palette and accents to guide attention; exact colours remain to be reviewed.
- **Lighting and readability:** soft, appealing lighting and readable contrast at the playable camera. The references include both bright and dark scenes; darkness or dramatic bloom is not a requirement for our courtyard.
- **Paper and feedback:** visible overlapping paper with varied formats and readable depth, a clear action target and an immediately noticeable change as paper comes away. The owner's reported missing next layer needs investigation before assuming the visual works. Use effects to reinforce the action without obscuring it.
- **Validation:** first produce a small integrated representative frame around the wall and held tool, inspect it from the actual FPS camera, and ask the owner to assess style, readability and appeal. Existing models, posters and the courtyard remain candidates, not approved final art. Screenshots are references only, not assets to copy or a mandate to adopt the referenced games' mechanics.

The impression should be randomly pasted and layered, rather than a sparse or uniform arrangement.
