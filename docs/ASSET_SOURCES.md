## Replacement right hand — 2026-11-06
`PeelRightArm.prefab` and `PeelRightArmMesh.asset` are derived from the Unity Technologies Starter Assets character already licensed and imported in the project. The right wrist is on the positive-X side of the complete character, opposite the left wrist. The derived mesh retains triangles weighted to the right forearm, hand and fingers. The game uses positive scale and retains the source finger rig. WRAD ARMS was removed from Resources and archived in SourceArt.

## First-person hand candidate — 2026-11-06
The derived `PeelHandSurface.asset` preserves the WRAD ARMS mesh and rig weights. Its triangles are divided into glove, cuff and sleeve slots according to wrist and finger weights. The three matte colors are authored for this project.

Follow-up: the WRAD ARMS FBX is now used as `Assets/JustOneMorePeel/Resources/PeelHand.fbx`. PeelToolView hides the left arm, poses the right fingers and assigns a matte URP material in place of the source skin texture. Each tool has its own placement. The latest captures are `Logs/stage2-hand-{card,scraper,steam}.png`. The steamer pose supports the tank from below. The original trial described below was superseded by this integration, which awaits owner review.

Existing Starter Assets contain a complete third-person character rather than a ready posed first-person hand. [WRAD ARMS by wriks](https://github.com/wwwriks/wrad-arms) is offered under CC0 1.0, including commercial use and modification, with attribution optional. Actual `arms.fbx`, `arms.blend`, `arm_albedo_pale.png` and `LICENSE` were downloaded to `SourceArt/HandCandidate`. Unity imported the skinned FBX and finger bones; the material and candidate were inspected with the playable camera. Manual test poses did not produce a convincing grip on both card and scraper; the forearm is visibly distorted and the model's retro texture clashes with the target direction. The candidate is not integrated or accepted. The temporary Unity import was removed; source and test frames in `Logs/hand-*.png` remain for further work. A separate medical teaching hand on Grab3D was rejected from its source description: it has joint markers and a display stand, not a player's hand.
